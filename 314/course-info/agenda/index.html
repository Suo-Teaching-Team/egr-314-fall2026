    Wednesday April 2

    • System verification
      • Review your block diagram assignment.
      • Review the process diagram.
      • There should be one message for at least one sensor. There should be a message that serves at least each motor. Permits the motor to be driven at a particular speed. To a particular location.
      • Your HMI should be able to send and receive data.
      • MQTT should be able to send and receive data.
      • Review the message table.
      • Table mapping between who sends what messages and who receives those messages.
      • Check for consistency in data type and length
      • We will be checked three subsystems.
        • Sensor motor controller.
        • HMI
        • MQTT web server.
        • We will also be checking for the percentage of messages that are supported.
      • Because it is important for each person on the team to meet the class specification, There is a points calculation each team needs to play in order to calculate the maximum number of points they could achieve.
      • If one person on the team will cause the entire team to get zero messages passed, It's in the team's best interest to not penny that person to join the team for checkoff.
      • In that case, the individual will have to check off directly with an instructor. Teams will not get the points from the required subsystem
      • In that case, also, the individual API will become very important as it is the documentation for the individual to check off.
      • We will be needing two new charts.
      • One is the controller diagram.
      • The other is the software diagram.
    • Final demonstration.
      • We will be checking to make sure that you have Met the Course requirements.
      • We will also be checking for quality of build and reliability.
      • Quality of HMI.
