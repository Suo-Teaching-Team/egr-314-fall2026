    Block Diagram,

    Team Assignment

    Objectives

    The goal of this assignment is to determine and document the manner in which each team member's project will communicate electronically It is common practice in industry to document the logical flow of software prior to handling implementation-level details like coding. These diagrams make writing code much easier, as well as help to improve the quality of your software design. This will be accomplished by establishing:

    A team-level block diagram highlighting the Subsystems interfacing with the ESP32

    Resources

    • draw.io package (compatible with Google Drive)

    Assignment

    Team Block Diagram

    Starting with the example from the project description, as well as your teammates' individual block diagrams, provide a detailed team-level block diagram

    1. Your goal is to show:
      1. Subsystem Owner, for each teammate (Each team member must be in charge of 1 Subsystem)
      2. Sensors connections (I2C, SPI)
      3. Power Connection and Distribution
      4. ICSP or USB Programming Port
      5. Map all pins that will be used by the team to specific gpio / peripheral pins on each microcontroller.
      6. if I2C, what is the address of each "peripheral"?
      7. if SPI, which pins are used for "chip select"?
      8. details of peripheral ICs, such as sensors, actuators, and OLEDs, which must be directly connected to the individual's microcontroller.

    _Figure: Example team-level block diagram,

    Homework Preparation and Submission

    This work will be used -- and graded -- in multiple ways. It will be checked for completeness on the date given in Canvas.

    Preparation

    Please prepare this assignment as a new page on your team's living report website. Assemble:

    Block Diagram

    Assemble this into a new page on your team's website called "Block Diagram

    Once completed,

    • Create a link to this new page on the datasheet's main landing page.
    • Check the link to ensure that it works
    • export this new page as a pdf

    The new page representing your assignment should not present as a page of links. Do not use it to link to other documents especially living documents, such as google docs, draw.io drawings, or google sheets. Rather, contents from other documents should be exported, saved in your repository, and hosted natively in markdown (or if need be html) on the site itself.

    Submission

    To submit a complete assignment, please submit:

    • A working URL to the new "Block Diagram
    • the exported PDF for easier review

    Both items must be submitted, by the deadline in the Canvas course calendar, in order to receive full credit. It is your responsibility to ensure that your submission to Canvas was successful. Late Canvas submissions will be graded per the policy in the syllabus.

    Subsequent Uses of this Document

    This document will be used by your teammates to help make decisions on how to distribute functionality across your teammates. Additionally, as part of your living report website -- a public website -- this document should be updated and improved throughout the semester in order to reflect your most current design. It will also be reviewed seen by external reviewers who will be given the opportunity to provide direct and indirect feedback, and as part of your team's final report.

    Checklist

    • The page is easy to find
    • The block diagram is clear and well-formatted
    • Arrows are shown
    • The team's power plan is shown through the use of connectors and / or jumpers
    • Each teammate's role is clearly documented with corresponding Subsystem
