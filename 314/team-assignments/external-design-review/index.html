    External Design Review

    This assignment has components that are graded at both the team and individual level. Please read through the assignment carefully.

    Introduction

    A design review is a common industry process by which a design is critiqued by colleagues with the intent of discovering errors and suggestions of improvements prior to implementation.

    In this activity, your team will both (1) present and discuss your design and (2) provide feedback on others' designs by asking questions and completing a detailed evaluation checklist.

    External design review presentations will be presented on Monday and Wednesday in Cooley Ballroom A, as noted in the course calendar. Do not come to the classroom on these days.

    The objectives of this design review are to:

    1. Update the living project report document and populate it with deliverables for grading.
    2. Update the virtual collaboration space where your team shares design documents.
    3. Present your project in its current form.
    4. Receive detailed feedback on your team's progress.

    Format

    Design reviews will span over two days of class. On each day, half of the teams in the class will present their designs for feedback for 25 minutes, twice, to two different groups of students, faculty, and/or industry professionals. The other half of the teams will review the teams that are presenting. Teams that are not presenting will provide feedback through the review forms linked above.

    Design reviews will be in-person in Cooley Ballroom A. Attendance will be taken on both design review days.

    Dress is business casual on both days of design reviews.

    Presentations will consist of talking reviewers through the design documents found on their team websites. Reviewers can ask questions both during and after your presentation. Do NOT create a PowerPoint presentation.

    You will not get credit for the presentation portion of this assignment if you are not present during your day to present.

    Assignment

    Team Report Website Preparation

    During the presentation, you may use your team report website and individual datasheets as an outline for your discussion. We will not be providing access to projectors or monitors, so the website will be your only shared visual aid.

    Improve your team report website so that you can use it to efficiently walk through your design discussion:

    • Home page (index.md)
      • Add an introduction to the homepage

    • Team Organization

      • Use formatting to highlight the mission statement and charter from the surrounding paragraphs so we can identify it easily.

        Hint: the right karat (>) helps to emphasize passages like this one!

    • Team Concept Generation and Design Ideation

      • Ensure that your concept generation video is embedded(not linked) and playable directly from within the page.
      • Update to reflect any new knowledge you have gained in user needs, other competitive products, or changes in your project direction.
      • Update ideation based on feedback received since the initial submission. No specific action items are required, but continue to add detail based on feedback
      • Include a short summary of the decision-making process your team used to pick one of the team's design concepts. Did you further combine good ideas from your design ideation? Did you select the best? How was the decision made?
      • If the selected design deviates significantly from the design ideation section, please explain the process by which you updated it.
    • Team Block Diagram, Protocol, and Message Structure
      • Discuss your team's design and decision-making process related to this section
      • Update your block diagram as needed to reflect changes to your current design, making sure you address any feedback received, whether in person or in writing.
      • Explain your decision-making process for how you developed this and explain how your block diagram meets your product requirements

    Individual datasheet Website Preparation

    Update your individual "datasheet" website so that you can use it to efficiently walk through your:

    • Individual Block Diagram
      • Update your block diagram as needed to reflect changes to your current design, making sure you address any feedback received, whether in person or in writing.
    • Component Selection
      • Update component selections based on decisions made since the initial submission. Take into account suggestions made by the teaching team and based on the evolution of your project.
      • Ensure you fully explain your decision-making process and how your selected components meet your product requirements
    • Schematic Design
      • Ensure that a rasterized image (png/jpg) is visible on the schematic design page itself
      • Ensure that a high-resolution PDF version of your individual schematic is linked to
      • Ensure your Cadence project files are zipped and linked to
      • Power Budget: Create your power budget to reflect your current design See original assignment
      • Explain how the power budget has helped to estimate power needs and any conclusions you have come to
      • Discuss how the functionality of this schematic satisfies user needs and product requirements through an in-depth discussion of function.

    In addition, ensure that all pages implement the following improvements:

    • Update or improve the flow of your websites to make it more navigable
    • Check to ensure that all links are working, that the website is easily navigable from the homepage
    • Ensure that all individual datasheets are easily accessible from the team website.
    • Please integrate any feedback you collected from the teaching team, or from your team assignment grades.
    • Please ensure older assignments are consistent with the current direction of your project
    • Please update or improve the flow of your report to make it more navigable
    • All images are visible on the page itself (rather than as a link)

    Outline

    Consider the following timeline for your presentations:

    1. Team Welcome & Introduction (30 seconds)
    2. Concept Generation & Design Ideation (2 minutes)
      1. Highlight Key takeaways and updated design concept
    3. Team Block Diagram, Protocol, and Message Structure (2 minutes)
      1. Highlight team connection order and any special connections needed
      2. Highlight message passing sequence diagram
      3. Identify key messages passed between teammates.
    4. Individual Datasheet ( 4 min x 4)
      1. Block Diagram & Component Selection (1 minute)
        1. Highlight key components & serial communication selected
      2. Schematic (3 min)
    5. Q & A (4 minutes)
    6. Final Notes and Thoughts (2 minutes)

    Homework Preparation and Submission

    This work will be used -- and graded -- in multiple ways. It will graded based on the date and time submitted in Canvas.

    Preparation

    In preparing for the design review, you must make changes both to

    • your team report website
    • each teammate's individual "datasheet" website

    Once completed, copy the main url of

    • your team report website
    • each teammate's individual "datasheet" website

    And submit each to their respective assignment submission pages.

    Your report and datasheet websites should not present as a page of links. Do not use it to link to other documents especially living documents, such as google docs, draw.io drawings, or google sheets. Rather, contents from other documents should be exported, saved in your repository folder structure, and hosted natively in markdown (or if need be html) on the site itself.

    Qualitative Assessment

    This document will assessed for quality by the teaching team when reviewed during the external design review. Before then, please engage with the teaching team during office hours and/or classtime to review this document for ways to improve it. We are happy to provide feedback, which will be your responsibility to integrate and address before the external design review.

    Qualitative grading will assess how closely the team follows instructions, for general formatting, layout, organization, and legibility, and to ensure feedback-based updates were applied to all sections. Items above will be graded based on the following scale (please see the syllabus for a full description of each):

    • 100% Exceeds Expectations
    • 85% Above Expectations
    • 70% Meets Expectations
    • 55% Below Expectations
    • 40% Does Not Meet Expectations
    • 0 Missing / Not Submitted

    Subsequent Uses of this Document

    As part of your individual datasheet and team report website -- a public website -- this document should be updated and improved throughout the semester in order to reflect your most current design. It will be reviewed by external reviewers, who will be given the opportunity to provide direct and indirect feedback. It will be reviewed, in revised form, as part of your team's final report.
