## Objectives

The goal of this assignment is to introduce you to the the Microchip PIC and the MPLabX IDE, and to verify that you can create a project, add a peripheral (GPIO), configure it, program it, and flash a LED

## Prior to the Demonstration of Proficiency

1. Complete this [Tutorial](https://embedded-systems-design.github.io/mplabx-project-setup/) for creating a new, simple project in MPLabX on the Embedded Systems Design website.
1. Complete this [Tutorial](https://embedded-systems-design.github.io/icsp-tutorial/) for setting up a Snap Programmer on the Embedded Systems Design website.

## Demonstration of Proficiency

1. Connect your Programmer to a computer via USB. Open your MPLabX project. Generate MCC. Compile the program, and program your board.
1. Demonstrate that the external LED changes state once a second.

**A live demonstration by the end of class is required. No late demonstrations will be accepted.**

## Submission

No Canvas submission is necessary. Complete your demonstrations by the deadline in the course calendar on [Canvas](https://canvas.asu.edu). Late demonstrations will be graded per the policy in the syllabus.
