## Introduction

A _design review_ is a common industry process by which a design is critiqued by colleagues with the intent of discovering errors and suggestions of improvements prior to implementation.

In this activity, your team will both (1) present and discuss your design and (2) provide feedback on others' designs by asking questions and completing a detailed evaluation checklist.

External design review presentations will be presented on Monday and Wednesday in Cooley Ballroom A, as noted in the course calendar. **Do not come to the classroom on these days.**

The objectives of this design review are to:

1. Update the living project report document and populate it with deliverables for grading.
1. Update the virtual collaboration space where your team shares design documents.
1. Present your project in its current form.
1. Receive detailed feedback on your team's progress.

## Format

Design reviews will span over two days of class. On each day, half of the teams in the class will present their designs for feedback for 25 minutes, twice, to two different groups of students, faculty, and/or industry professionals. The other half of the teams will review the teams that are presenting. Teams that are not presenting will provide feedback through the review forms linked above.

Design reviews will be **in-person in Cooley Ballroom A.** Attendance will be taken on both design review days.

**Dress is business casual on both days of design reviews.**

Presentations will consist of talking reviewers through the design documents found on their team websites. Reviewers can ask questions both during and after your presentation. **Do NOT create a PowerPoint presentation.**

> You will not get credit for the presentation portion of this assignment if you are not present during your day to present.

## Assignment

### Team Report Website Preparation

During the presentation, you may use your team report website and individual datasheets as an outline for your discussion.  We will not be providing access to projectors or monitors, so the website will be your only shared visual aid.

Improve your team report website so that you can use it to efficiently walk through your design discussion:

* Home page (index.md)
    * Add an introduction to the homepage
* Team Organization
    * Use formatting to highlight the mission statement and charter from the surrounding paragraphs so we can identify it easily.

        > **Hint:** the right karat (```>```) helps to emphasize passages like this one!

* Team Concept Generation and Design Ideation
    * Ensure that your concept generation video is embedded(not linked) and playable directly from within the page.
    * Update to reflect any new knowledge you have gained in user needs, other competitive products, or changes in your project direction.
    * Update ideation based on feedback received since the initial submission. No specific action items are required, but continue to add detail based on feedback
    * Include a short summary of the decision-making process your team used to pick one of the team's design concepts. Did you further combine good ideas from your design ideation? Did you select the best? How was the decision made?
    * If the selected design deviates significantly from the design ideation section, please explain the process by which you updated it.
* Team Block Diagram, Protocol, and Message Structure
    * Discuss your team's design and decision-making process related to this section
    * Update your block diagram as needed to reflect changes to your current design, making sure you address any feedback received, whether in person or in writing.
    * Explain your decision-making process for how you developed this and explain how your block diagram meets your product requirements
