## Public Demonstration at Innovation Showcase

During the Innovation Showcase, your entire team must be present to demonstrate your project to industry visitors and the general public.

## Grading

> Demonstrations will be accepted during Innovation Showcase. We will be grading presentations based on the:

* Quality/Professionalism of Demonstration

* Problem Statement Overview - Introduce the problem you are trying to solve and why it needs to be solved, make sure its a high level overview and give enough background to the problem. 

* User Needs Overview - Introduce the user needs that were developed early on in the semester. Also explain how they tie in or relate to your problem Statement.   

* Block Diagram Overview - Walk the teaching team through your block diagram and how it is able to fulfill the user needs. 

* Demo of Project - Show the teaching your hardware functioning (Sensors, Actuators, LEDS, Speakers) This is a chance to show off your projects features. 

* Ability to answer questions asked by the teaching team regarding your project. be prepared to discuss details regarding the topics.





  *   Each grading Criteria described above will be count for 40 points. Each member of the team must be present and speak during the Innovation Showcase demonstration. Please keep your presentations under 20 minuets so that the teaching team can have enough time to grade and speak with all the teams.
