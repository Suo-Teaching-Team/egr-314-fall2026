## Poster

Create a poster using one of the **required PowerPoint poster templates** (above). A body text size of 18 points or larger is recommended for readability.

1. **Title Bar**. Project name/title and team members; Included in PowerPoint templates: course number, name, and semester,
1. Pane 1: **Concept Sketch** -- Include Concept Sketch from "Concept Generation and Design Ideation" assignment
1. Pane 2: **Text about Concept Sketch** -- Include Mission Statement and Charter from "Team Organization" assignment.  From "Concept Generation and Design Ideation" assignment, identify your audience, and critical user needs of museum visitors.
1. Pane 3: **Team Block Diagram**: Resize it to fit as needed, while keeping the aspect ratio intact
1. Pane 4: **Text about Team Block Diagram**: Indicate individual roles of each teammate, and major components selected for each subsystem.
1. Pane 5: **Communication Sequence Diagram**: Include your final communication sequence diagram.
1. Pane 6: **Text about Communication Sequence Diagram**: Include a description of each message type and how they are passed to each teammate.
1. Pane 7: **Picture of System**: Include a high quality image of the four PCBs (standalone or integrated into a final assembly)
1. The poster will also be judged on the quality and use of visuals

***Please adhere strictly to the topics listed above.***

Submit your final poster **in PDF format** (required) for printing to the [Print and Imaging Lab](https://print.asu.edu/print-lab/student-showcases/innovation-showcase) by the deadline noted. **No late submissions will be accepted.**

### Canvas Submission

Submit your final poster in PDF format to this assignment on Canvas by the deadline in Canvas. Do not submit links to Google documents. It is your responsibility to ensure that your submission to Canvas was successful. Late Canvas submissions will be graded per the policy in the syllabus. No credit will be awarded for assignments not submitted to Canvas.
